About

Hello, I'm Luca

I'm Luca, a final-year MEng Aerospace Engineering student at the University of Bath, graduating in July 2027 with a predicted First. I'm Italian and have lived in Geneva since 2009.

My year in industry was split between two placements. At ChargePoint Technology in Liverpool I designed equipment around pharmaceutical containment valves in SolidWorks and automated the company's product database. At Andel in Barnsley I redesigned enclosures for leak-detection and flood-protection products, including a gully flood sensor approved for commercial use and a self-initiated redesign of the flagship BundGuard. In summer 2026 I worked in continuing airworthiness at Avia Seven and Marathon Airlines in Athens, assessing Service Bulletins and maintenance programmes for Embraer jets.

At university I was project manager and technical integrator of VA-55, a hybrid VTOL crop-spraying drone. This year my final-year project investigates serrated wingtip devices, using Ansys CFD and wind tunnel testing.

Outside engineering I design LEGO models, play volleyball, and record and produce my own music.
