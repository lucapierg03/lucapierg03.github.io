Tailfin @Home Design Challenge

Tailfin · @Home Design Challenge (University of Bath, Year 1) · June 2023

Context

The brief was an adjustable version of Tailfin's AeroPack seat-post-to-pack connector. A 20-point product design specification covered function, mounting, strength, manufacture and user experience.

What I learned

My first design project with my own requirements: I talked to cyclist friends to shape the requirements and the design. Because we handed in slides without presenting them, I learnt to show a full design process so that it could be understood on its own.

Role
Designer
Result
Two final designs: a 128 g tool-less connector and a lower-cost ratcheted version.
Skills
Concept selection (MCDA)CADDesign for manufactureHand calculations

Brief and specification

Concept selection (MCDA)

Each concept was scored against the datum (Concept 5) on every criterion. Concepts 4 and 6 scored highest, matching the intuitive choice made before the analysis, so both became final designs: Concept 4 is tool-less but costlier, Concept 6 cheaper but less elegant.

Final design: Concept 4

A 128 g aluminium connector, tool-less, with ±90° of motion at the seat post and 180° at the pack bracket. Every part except the quick-release clamps can be machined, like the existing Tailfin part, and the bending stress was checked against the 40 MPa limit.

Final design: Concept 6

A lower-cost alternative whose length is set through ratcheted holes. The connecting beam sits in the middle with a ratcheted mounting beam on each side, which simplified the design and cut cost. At full extension the bending stress is 11.4 MPa including a safety factor, against the 40 MPa limit.
