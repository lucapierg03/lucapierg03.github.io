Wing design: mass-optimised composite wing panel

University of Bath · Wing Design (Year 3) · Autumn 2025

Context

I used MATLAB to mass-optimise a Saab 340 wing panel, accounting for design loads, reserve factors, panel geometry and laminate construction.

What I did

  • Wrote the optimisation loop: a design passed only when every reserve factor was above 1.
  • Wrote a function that picks the optimal stacking sequence for each panel element, following industry laminate rules.
  • The code generated the final model automatically.
Role
Individual project
Result
72% (First). The laminate design is 25% lighter per unit area than the aluminium panel.
