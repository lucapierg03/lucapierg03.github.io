LEGO Rolls-Royce Trent XWB replica

Personal project · Designed in BrickLink Studio

Context

A fully LEGO-built replica of the Trent XWB, recreating the fan, compressor and turbine stages, the internal engine structure and the cowling. Building it taught me a lot about how the engine works inside.

What I did

  • Designed every part in BrickLink Studio.
  • Built an openable cowling and a working thrust reverser.
  • Modelled the shaft with the fan, compressor and turbine stages, and the stator vanes.
Role
Designer and builder
Result
Over 1,000 pieces, designed in about a week.
