Gully flood sensor: new product

The prototype took so long to install that roads had to be closed. I redesigned the enclosure for quick installation with few tools and no specialist staff, and for easy opening during maintenance. It was field-tested on a gully and approved for commercial use.

CellarGuard enclosure and drilling jig

I redesigned the CellarGuard enclosure for easier assembly, keeping it compatible with the old mounting and the next BundGuard probes. I also designed jigs that standardise hand-machined parts, and wrote build documents for Production.

SLS-printed enclosures: FlowStop 2 and a circuitry box

Turning the valve horizontal gave FlowStop 2 a larger flat back for adhesive mounting, and the contoured base means the valve only fits the right way round and no longer rattles. It is about 20% more compact, printed in-house instead of outsourced, and was shown at Andel's 2025 Distributors' Conference.

Water-quality sensor with Tecnológico de Monterrey, Mexico

An early-stage product with changing requirements. I fitted every sensor into a bollard that moves up and down with the water level, designed the bollard changes around the supplier's limited tooling, and approximated PCB and connector sizes that were not yet designed.
