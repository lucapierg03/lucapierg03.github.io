Shaft design: layshaft for a pump drive

University of Bath · Shaft Design (Year 2) · 2023 – 24

Context

Design the layshaft of a two-stage belt drive that takes an electric motor at 1,440 rpm down to 150 rpm for a multi-cylinder piston pump delivering 3 litres per second.

What I did

  • Worked out the reduction ratios and the shear force, bending moment and torque along the shaft.
  • Chose the belts, pulleys and bearings and iterated the shaft layout until it would not fail under the design loads.
  • Built the CAD model in Inventor and produced the part and assembly drawings.
Role
Pair project
Result
74% (First). The final design was checked for failure under every loading condition.
